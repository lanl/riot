## What this PR proposes

Add ideal MHD to RIOT as a new `mhd` package, ported from `lanl/artemis` branch
`dempsey/mhd` at `3e5aeb5` (artemis PR #123):

- A face-centered magnetic field evolved by Gardiner–Stone upwind constrained transport.
- HLLE, HLLD and LLF MHD Riemann solvers (`hydro/riemann = mhd_hlle | mhd_hlld | mhd_llf`).
- `B²/(2μ₀)` included in the conserved total energy only when `physics/mhd = true`.
  Default is off. One unit parameter, `mhd/mu0`, defaults to `4π`.
- Problem generators: Brio–Wu, field loop (2D and 3D), Orszag–Tang, and the circularly
  polarized Alfvén wave.
- Supported configuration: Cartesian, uniform grid, single material, ideal gas, MPI.
  Anything else is rejected at startup.
